# FK Furniture & Interior palette preview prompts

All four use the same composition: tall desktop homepage crop with the brand/navigation header and hero, then Selected Projects as one large hospitality image on the left and category index on the right. Hero copy: “Spaces with” followed by italic “a point of view.”; “Interior design, bespoke furniture and turnkey execution for homes, workplaces and hospitality spaces.”; buttons “Explore our work” and “Discuss your project”. Header links: About, Services, Projects, Contact. Project section: “SELECTED PROJECTS”, “Selected projects”, “All projects →”, Hospitality / Residential / Commercial. Natural warm luxury living room in hero and hotel lounge in project image; photos remain naturally colored. Editorial serif headline, sans body, generous whitespace. No added sections, project card grid, client claims or watermark. Small legend below site states each 60/30/10 palette.
