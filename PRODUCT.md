## Users

- Hospitality buyers: hotel and resort owners, developers, procurement teams, and operators seeking bespoke furniture and interior fit-outs for guest rooms, lobbies, restaurants, banquets, and related spaces.
- Architects, interior designers, and contract buyers supplying drawings, specifications, samples, or a bill of quantities (BOQ) for custom manufacturing and execution.
- Residential clients seeking complete home interiors, custom furniture, renovations, or upgrades.
- Office and commercial clients seeking functional interiors and furniture suited to their organization.

The company profile covers residential, commercial, and hospitality work. The homepage and service briefs place greater emphasis on B2B hospitality manufacturing. The relative priority of these audiences remains open; do not silently narrow the company to hotels alone.

## Product Purpose

Help prospective clients understand FK's interior and furniture work, assess relevant projects and capabilities, and start a consultation or project enquiry. The supplied briefs describe a portfolio and enquiry website with quotation requests, company-profile/catalogue downloads, and direct contact options.

The intended commercial outcome is a relevant enquiry that gives the team enough context to discuss the project and prepare a quotation. The briefs do not establish an online retail checkout or a public fixed-price catalogue.

## Positioning

The company profile describes a Gurgaon-based interior design and bespoke furniture business with its own manufacturing setup and experience from design through execution and installation. Its stated distinction combines practical site experience, customized furniture, factory-made precision, and single-point responsibility for turnkey work.

The family business began with labour-based work in Gurgaon in 1986. Operations later expanded to Goa; material supply alongside execution was introduced in 2012. The profile says the professional company was formalized later. Describe 1986 as the start of the family craft legacy, not a verified incorporation date for the current legal entity.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients evaluate completed work, service scope, materials and finishes, production capabilities, and suitability for their project. Hospitality and contract enquiries may include a BOQ, architectural plans, drawings, project location, and new-build or renovation requirements.

The proposed delivery workflow is consultation and drawing/BOQ review, material and sample or mock-up approval, manufacturing and quality checks, then packing, delivery, and on-site installation. This is the service workflow described by the briefs, not an implemented website workflow.

The company profile lists Gurgaon and Goa offices and names a completed turnkey hospitality project at Resort De Coracao, Tapovan, Rishikesh. The briefs propose pan-India delivery and installation. Do not interpret this as an unconditional service or delivery guarantee for every location.
